Safety Takeaways

  • Both judges are conservative — they err toward rejecting, which is safer in clinical context
  • GPT-5.2 has lowest dangerous error rate (8.2%) — better for safety-critical screening
  • MedGemma is less strict (24.0% vs 29.8% FN) — catches more valid alternatives
  • Complementary strengths: Using both judges and flagging disagreements maximizes safety
  • AI judges as screening tools, not final arbiters — physician review remains essential
