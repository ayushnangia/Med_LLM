Key Contributions

  • MedGemma dual role: Best predictor (75.4%, 6.1/9) AND best judge (kappa=0.675)
  • Novel task: MedGemma adapted as structured medical judge
  • Real clinical validation: 69 German tumor board cases, not synthetic benchmarks
  • 100% expert coverage: Board-certified uro-oncologist reviewed all 1,242 outputs
  • Bias discovery: Statistically significant self-judging bias (p=0.017)
  • Production system: Live web app with Supabase, deployed on Vercel
